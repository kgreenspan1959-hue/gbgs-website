Three problems, and what fixing them looks like

Problem 1

Quotes that go quiet

You drive out, write it up, and nobody follows up.

Every open quote sits in one list, and the office gets told which ones are about to expire. The customer can pick an option and accept online.

Problem 2

Friday time cards

Chasing a stack of paper and texts every week.

Each tech fills in the week, a supervisor approves it, and it prints ready for payroll.

Problem 3

Did the job make money?

Usually you find out when the accountant tells you.

On a project, you see the budget, the parts and the hours side by side.

How I'd start

Every shop runs differently. I'd start by asking how yours works today, what you're tired of chasing, and which tools you need to keep. Fifteen minutes, no pitch deck.
