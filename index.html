How We Work

Plain rules, kept without exception.

The Operator Behind It

Kevin Greenspan is not a consultant who read about business in a book. For thirty-four years he has run a second-generation healthcare practice in the North Carolina mountains — his father opened it in 1981; Kevin joined him in 1992 and built it into a practice in a town of 8,000 that grossed $2 million a year out of 2,000 square feet. In a cash practice there's nowhere to hide: if the phone wasn't answered, the schedule wasn't full, and the follow-up didn't happen, that was his problem, personally, that night. He got good at systems because he had to. Before the practice: building a car-detailing operation in South Florida, surviving a rental-property loss in 2009 that taught more than any MBA could, and finishing an eight-year degree — undergrad and graduate school stacked on top of each other — in six and a half years, while tutoring organic chemistry to undergraduates and starting to raise a family, because tuition was money and money was earned.

Today he works with established business owners across Western North Carolina — especially owners thinking about what their business becomes when they're ready to step back — bringing modern systems to Main-Street companies without the Silicon Valley nonsense.

The rule that governs everything: if we wouldn't run it in our own family's businesses, we don't put it in yours. We built the first of these for our own family's businesses before we built for anyone else. That's the proof we started with.
